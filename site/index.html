    What's a t‑stat?

    The t‑stat compares a strategy's average return with how noisy its returns are: t = mean ÷ (standard deviation ÷ √n), where n is the number of returns.

    On the chart, that is how far the line ends above zero, measured in units of standard deviation × √n, which is how far luck alone typically carries a strategy that makes nothing. The scale shown on reveal counts these units.

    As a rule of thumb, a t‑stat above about 2 is unlikely to come from luck alone.
